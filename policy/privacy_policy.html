Bird Care Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026
100% offline. Your data stays on your device.

Bird Care (the "App") respects your privacy. This policy explains how the App works and what data it does and does not collect.

1. Personal data

We do not collect personal data.

  • The App works 100% offline on your device.
  • We do not ask you to create an account or sign in with email, a phone number, or a social network.
  • Bird profiles, weights, photo diary entries, reminders, and breeding records are stored in a local SQLite database on your phone.
  • Nothing is uploaded to a server or shared with a third party.

2. Device permissions

To provide bird-care features, the App may request these permissions:

  • Photo library / camera: Used only when you choose a bird photo or attach photos to a diary entry. Photos stay in the App’s private storage on the device.
  • Local notifications: Used to remind you to feed, change water, and clean the cage at the times you set. Notifications are scheduled on the phone and do not go through a push server.

3. Backup and restore

The App can export and import a backup file (.zip). Where you store that file — Google Drive, a USB drive, or a computer — is entirely up to you.

4. Google Play policy

The App follows Google Play’s rules for user privacy and data.
